# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Operations and procurement managers who supervise autonomous purchasing agents.
They need to understand exceptions quickly, approve or reject exact orders, and
trace every decision. Buyer and supplier agents interact through the API and
appear in the interface as accountable actors, not chatbots.

## Product Purpose

Mandate lets AI agents buy for a business without giving them unlimited
authority. It verifies identity, applies supplier and spend policy, pauses
exceptions for human approval, processes approved payments, and preserves an
auditable transaction history.

## Positioning

Mandate is the trust and transaction layer for agent-to-agent commerce.

## Brand Personality

Assured, exacting, editorial. The product should make governed automation feel
legible and calm, even when a purchase needs immediate attention.

## Anti-references

No generic SaaS card wall, AI gradients, chatbot shell, dark fintech terminal,
glass effects, vanity analytics, or decorative automation diagrams. The
Garriock & Co. reference supplies the typographic scale, monochrome rhythm,
hairline rules, and confident whitespace—not its agency content.

## Design Principles

- Make authority visible: always show who acts, for which organization, and
  under which mandate.
- Make exceptions faster to judge than to misunderstand.
- Show the exact order and policy evidence before presenting an action.
- Treat the audit trail as product value, not technical exhaust.
- Use typography and spacing for hierarchy; decoration must earn its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA, preserve full keyboard operation and visible focus, never
encode status by color alone, keep touch targets at least 44px, and provide a
reduced-motion path for every transition.
